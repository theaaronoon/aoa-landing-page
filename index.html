Questions

Is this session chargeable?

No. The session and your plan cost nothing, and there's no obligation. If you later decide to buy or sell with us, we'll explain any fees upfront before you commit to anything.

Am I obligated to buy anything?

No. You'll get honest numbers and a clear plan, even if the right answer is to wait. The plan is yours to keep whether or not you work with us.

I'm a year or more away from buying, or my HDB hasn't reached MOP. Is it too early?

It's the best time. Planning early means your finances, loan and timeline are ready when you're eligible, instead of rushing a decision later.

What should I prepare before the session?

Just a rough idea of your budget, income, CPF balance and any existing loans. If you own a property now, have its details handy too. Don't worry if you don't have everything; we'll work through it together.

Is it online or in person?

Your first session is online: a 60-minute Google Meet call you can join from anywhere, with the link sent when you book. Any sessions after that can be in person.
